The garden

Fresh air, green space and room to celebrate.

Sweet Memories Gardens is an outdoor events garden just off Thika Road. Couples, families and organisers book it for the day, and spend it surrounded by nature.

You bring the occasion; the garden gives you the setting. Send us your date and roughly how many guests are coming, and we will reply with whether it is free and what you need to know to book.

Check a date
Before you book, ask us about
  • How many guests the garden holds comfortably
  • Rates and what is included in the hire
  • Tents, chairs, tables and sound
  • Parking, timings and catering rules
Events we host

Whatever you are celebrating, there is space for it.

The garden is open for any kind of event. These are the ones people ask about most.

Weddings

Ceremony, reception or both, under open sky with room for your guests.

Birthdays

Milestone birthdays and family parties with space to spread out.

Graduations

A proper celebration for the graduate, the family and friends.

Showers & more

Bridal and baby showers, picnics, church days and team events.
